<h2>Warning: unfinished work and possible problems.</h2> 
<b>Potential short circuit</b><br>
The ways on the PCB from the XT30 power connector on the other side may short-circuit the metal cover on the Arduino USB-B connector. I recommend covering it with insulating tape.<br>
<br>
<b>Forced installation</b><br>
Some manufacturers of the 2EDGVC 5.00mm connector make it a tenth of a millimeter wider, so you have to force them all in at once.<br>
<br>
If you want to install a heat sink, you have to avoid the side of the Arduino with analog inputs and move it to the side so that the heat sink fits.
